# In Between Chaos and Equilibrium

***

## Project Name
In Between Chaos and Equilibrium

## Description
IBCE is a First Person Shooter-Platforming game hybrid, combining the best aspects of 80s and 90s 2 dimensional platforming games and 90s and early 2000s 3 dimensional first person shooter games together. This game combines the retro and the modern, the futuristic and ancient, the old and the new, and the best parts of gaming together. 

## Plot
In a futuristic 306 AD, a war of demons and aliens comes to Earth and kills many and destroyed a lot of the planet. These aliens hate God
for allowing demons to exist and want to rid the universe of all life, spirit, and divinity. 

These aliens have killed government officials in the Roman Empire and then took on their forms to pose as them to destroy humanity from within. The only way to tell for certain who is real is with Christian sacraments: Baptism and the Eucharist.

The princess of Egypt during the chaos, Kleopatra, has taken in interest in a war-hero-scholar named Andreas who she hopes can be a suitable husband and unlike her previous husbands that she has been matched with and also help her rid the world of these shapeshifting aliens. Unfortuately her father has a built in protolcol in her cybernetics that cause sedatives from her nanobots to be released into her bloodstream if she has been gone from her palace's ground for more than 24 hours. The sedatives will be released in 120 hours when the protocol starts. Andreas thankfully knows someone who can stop the protocol and prevent it from working, but they are all the way in Syria Phoenice. So Kleopatra, Andreas, her silly wine aunt, and Kleopatra's guards all fly over to the isolated province!
