# In Between Chaos and Equilibrium Episode 1

***

## Project Name
In Between Chaos and Equilibrium Episode 1: Life is Peachy

## Description
IBCE is a First Person Shooter-Platforming game hybrid, combining the best aspects of 80s and 90s 2 dimensional platforming games and 90s and early 2000s 3 dimensional first person shooter games together. This game combines the retro and the modern, the futuristic and ancient, the old and the new, and the best parts of gaming together. This game is a love letter to everything video games, their players, and to God himself.

## Plot
In 2074 AD, despite the warnings of many previous prophets, nobody prevented a massacre from happening. This massacre, caused by a pagan ritual done in the heart of the returned and rebuilt Babylonian Empire, was a demonic war waged against the alien isorropians brought to Earth and its solar system. The people who didn't perish in the initial attack who are of God or of his allies are now tasked with defeating the alien isorropians and demons and expelling them from humanity's solar system.

### Also, for new game developers who have just joined this project, here is a tutorial on how to get started with working on the game

Step 1: Install GIT by clicking this link: https://www.git-scm.com/download/win

Step 2: Install Godot 3.5 by clicking this link: https://godotengine.org/download/3.x/windows/ 

Step 3: When both applications are downloaded, go into file explorer and open the directory you want to save a clone of IBCE in.

Step 4: Then right click and click, "open bash here."

Step 5: Then type into the terminal: "git clone git@gitlab.com:pokefan1543/realibce.git" (without the quotation marks of course).

Congradulations! You have now gotten a copy of the game onto your computer so you can work on everything! 

Now, make sure that any time you make changes to the game, you never make them in the master branch. Only Noah Lanier can set what is in the master branch. Always make changes in another branch than master. If you watched the videos, you would know how to make a new branch.

## Roadmap For The Next Four Years:

### Episode 1

Playable Character: Kleopatra Megalou (Controlling the robot Axel-1A) (Plays like a mix of Mega Man X and the protagonist of Quake)

Major Characters: Klimentina Todorovski, Felix Lawson, Afrodisia “Maria” Megalou

Secondary Characters: Johnmark “Perry” Petit, Patrick Petit, Joakim Engelhart Jr., Minos Mikrogianakis

Miniboss: Ikenga “The Idol of Wood and Time”

Main Boss: Helios “The Sol Survivor”

Plot: Kleopatra is tasked with controlling a robot to do some recon over in an abandoned isorropian military base in the asteroid belt and destroy the enemy presence there.

Release Date: January 2025.

### Episode 2

Playable Character: Kleopatra Megalou (By herself) (She plays like a mix of classic Mega Man, Mega Man X, Samus with the shinespark if she could only go upwards with it, and the protagonist of Quake)

Major Character: Klimentina Todorovski

Secondary Characters: Ayşe Burakgazi, Joakim Engelhart Jr., Johnmark “Perry” Petit, Patrick Petit, Muhammad bin Mahmud bin Hamid, Isa bin Mahmud bin Hamid, Layla bint Yahya al-Rahshiq

Minibosses: Afrodisia “Maria” Megalou, Jorōgumo “Mistress of the Clever Abyss”, Esther Jacobsen (Secret Level Boss)

Main Boss: Venus “The Whore Whose Sons Persecuted the Apostles”

Plot: The base Kleopatra is stationed at is ambushed by Isorropians. Kleopatra and less than 10 others survive and all have to make their way to a spaceship to return to Earth to report what happened. Kleopatra at some point gets separated from the rest of the survivors.

Release Date: January 2026.

### Episode 3

Playable Character: Nehemiah A.J. Engelhart (Plays like a mix of Sonic the Hedgehog, the protagonist of Quake, Mega Man X, and Peppino Spaghetti from Pizza Tower)

Major Characters: Crisóstomo “Chrysostomus” Chacon, Athanasius Antibus, Hananiah Maksimovic, Kleopatra Megalou, Zaynab bint Zayn ad-Din al-Dimashqi, Zinoviy Makarov 

Secondary Characters: Chloe Chacon

Minibosses: Persephone Demetriou "The Deluded Death Witch" and Aphrodite “The Whore Whose Sons Persecuted the Apostles”, Kalypso “The Golden Haired Mistress”, The Twelve Olympians not including Hestia (each “god” will be faught in a different level and you will fight 2-3 at a time), Andromeda Aukema "Medusa" (fighting her is optional, but you in order to avoid fighting her, you have to do multiple extremely hard quick time events that are like the hardest levels in a rhythm game)

Main Boss: Baal “The Decayer of the Israelites” and Og Bar Hannibal

Plot: Nehemiah and his comrades all get sold into slavery for the Babylonian intellegence agency and slave trader organization, .E.B.A.U.M.E.R., and he and some of his other comrades are used as test subjects to test out portals for the agency. Wherever they lead to, they will leave to them die.

Release Date: January 2028.
